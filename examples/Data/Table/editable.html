juel-table editable

A grid of text to type into, such as a chart's data. The first row is the column headings. Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and the arrow keys move between cells; Tab on the last cell adds a row. + adds a column or a row; − (on hover or focus) removes one. Paste cells copied from a spreadsheet into a cell and they spread right and down.

The page's own controls go in the slots column-<i> (each heading) and row-<i> (each row's first cell); above, a colour swatch for each series.
