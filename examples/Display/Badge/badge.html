Badge

<juel-badge label="..."> renders a small pill, absolutely positioned at a corner (position, default ne) of its nearest positioned ancestor. Wrap it around (or alongside) content with position: relative — label supplies default text, or slot in your own content instead.

Types

The type attribute selects the semantic colour.

Render style

render-style="outline" swaps the filled background for a bordered, transparent one.

Position

All eight compass positions, anchored to the corner or edge of the wrapping element.

Slotted content

Slotted children override the label fallback.

★

On a button

A common real-world case: a notification count badge on a button, which already establishes its own positioned container.
