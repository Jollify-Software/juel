Particles

<juel-particles> fills its nearest positioned ancestor with falling or drifting particles: preset snow (default), rain, petals, feathers, confetti or bubbles, or your own image. emitter is an edge (top, bottom, left, right) or a point at x/y percent; direction (down, or degrees with 0 up and 90 right), spread, speed, size, density and wind (-1 to 1) adjust the preset. pause() and play() stop and start it.
