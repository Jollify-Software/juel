Frame

<juel-frame> hosts a page in a sandboxed iframe (sandbox="allow-scripts" by default) — a live preview of a Jetty deck, a Jellyfish page or a Juel Garden scene. Set srcdoc or src. With aspect="16:9" the page is laid out at design-width (1280) and scaled to fit. With buffered, new content loads behind and swaps in when ready (on load, or on the first message whose type is ready-type), so reloading never flashes.

send(data) posts to the page; its messages arrive as frame-message { data, pending, reply(data) }. frame-load fires when a page is shown.
