Shape — Hover Morph

hover turns a <juel-shape> into a hover reveal. On its own it morphs a plain rectangle into type; hover="<shape>" morphs type into that shape. Two geometric shapes animate the clip-path directly (the point lists are resampled to match, so it eases around the perimeter instead of jumping); anything involving an icon or <template> cross-fades instead. Tune it with --shape-morph-duration and --shape-morph-ease.
