juel-plan-editor

A floor plan as SVG (here in Parterre's annotations, two storeys). Click a shape to select it (Shift adds), drag on empty space for a box, drag the selection to move it (snapped to the grid; Alt: free), arrow keys to nudge, Delete to remove, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Wheel to zoom, middle button or Space+drag to pan, 0 to fit.
