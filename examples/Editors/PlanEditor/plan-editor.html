juel-plan-editor

A floor plan as SVG (here in Parterre's annotations, two storeys). Click a shape to select it (Shift adds), drag on empty space for a box, drag the selection to move it (snapped to the grid; Alt: free), arrow keys to nudge, Delete to remove, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Wheel to zoom, middle button or Space+drag to pan, 0 to fit. Drawing: Rect and Circle by dragging; Polygon and Polyline by clicking points (Enter, a double-click or the first point to finish; Backspace, Escape); Point by a click. Points snap to corners nearby, else the grid; Shift keeps 45°, Alt is free. One selected shape shows handles; double-click a polygon's edge to add a point, Alt+click a point to remove it.
