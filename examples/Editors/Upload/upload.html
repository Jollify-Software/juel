Upload

<juel-upload url="..." upload="..." multiple> is a click-to-browse and drag-and-drop file picker. Selecting files fires files-selected; with auto set, it immediately POSTs them as FormData to {url}/{upload}, firing upload-complete or upload-error. This page has no real backend, so it demonstrates selection and the resulting error event rather than a successful upload.
