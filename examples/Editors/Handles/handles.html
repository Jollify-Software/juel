juel-move-handle and juel-rotate-handle

Drag ✥ to move the box, or focus it and use the arrow keys (Shift for further). Drag ↻ round the box to turn it (Shift snaps to 15°), or focus it and use the arrow keys (→ ↑ clockwise, Shift for 15°). The handles only report what the pointer did: this page turns it into the box's translate and rotate.
