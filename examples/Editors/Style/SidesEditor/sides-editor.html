Sides Editor

<juel-sides-editor property="padding"> edits one length per side with an All | Top | Right | Bottom | Left toggle (a dot marks a side with its own value). It reads the shorthand and longhands from declarations and fires sides-changed { set, clear, values }: the shortest CSS, and the declarations to clear. The page applies it and passes the declarations back, as here. allow-auto and allow-negative suit margin.
