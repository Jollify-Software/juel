Steps

<juel-steps> is a step-progress indicator for wizard-style flows. Nest labelled <juel-step> panels for a self-contained wizard, or use total-steps alone as a bare numbered indicator you drive externally. Call next()/previous()/goTo(index), or click any step already reached.

Basic

Each <juel-step label="…"> becomes one indicator and one content panel. Completed steps are clickable.
