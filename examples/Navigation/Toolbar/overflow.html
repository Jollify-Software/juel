Toolbar: overflow

overflow keeps the toolbar to one row: what doesn't fit goes into a "…" menu at its end. A hidden button is clicked from there; a hidden menu's items are listed and fire its menu-select.

Priority

data-overflow-priority: the lowest goes first (unset is 0). Here Quote and Link (-1) go before anything else, and Insert and Image (1) stay longest, so the end of the row is kept.
