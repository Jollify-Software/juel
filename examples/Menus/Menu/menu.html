Menu

<juel-menu> opens its content from the element in slot="trigger". With trigger="click" a click outside or Escape closes it. Items are <juel-menu-item>s (or any element with role="menuitem"): choosing one fires menu-select and closes the menu. From the keyboard, the trigger opens it with focus on the first item, and the arrow keys, Home and End move between items.
