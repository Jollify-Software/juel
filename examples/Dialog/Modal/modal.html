Modal

<juel-modal> is a modal dialog on the native <dialog> element: it sits in the top layer, traps focus and makes the page behind it inert. open() shows it and close(value) closes it; modal-closed reports the value. Escape, the close button and a click on the backdrop dismiss it with '' (unless dismissible is false). On open, focus goes to the slotted element with autofocus.
