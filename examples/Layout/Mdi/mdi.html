Mdi

<juel-mdi> is a dependency-free docking layout, in the spirit of GoldenLayout: light-DOM children become panels, arranged as tabs (order="stack", the default) or side-by-side/stacked panes (order="row" / order="column"), resizable via <juel-splitter> and falling back to plain tabs on mobile regardless of order.

The workspace below is one juel-mdi instance wired up like a small IDE — an Explorer, an Editor, and a Console/Problems/Output pane group. Click a file to open it (this broadcasts a select-file message the Console listens for). Drag a pane's title bar onto another pane's edge to split it off, or onto its middle to merge it into that pane's tab stack — or drag it well clear of the whole layout to pop it straight into its own window, the same way you can with the toolbar button below. Each pane also has maximize and close buttons, and the whole layout can be saved/restored, popped into its own window, or reset.

IDE-style workspace (row, drag-to-dock, minimise/maximise, broadcast, popout, save/restore)

Every pane has minimise and maximise buttons (double-clicking a title bar also toggles maximise). A minimised panel becomes a tab on a bar above the layout; click the tab to put it back where it was. Both states are part of getLayout(), and layout-changed fires on every change; the JSON below updates live.
