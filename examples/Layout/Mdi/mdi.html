Mdi

<juel-mdi> lays out its light-DOM children as separate panels, each titled via a data-title attribute. order="stack" (the default) shows them as tabs; order="row" or order="column" arranges them side by side or stacked, resizable via <juel-splitter>. On a mobile-sized viewport it always falls back to tabs, regardless of order.
