Tooltips

window.juel.tips gives every element with a title (or data-tip) a Juel tooltip, in the page and inside shadow roots: after a short hover, at once on keyboard focus, or on a long press on touch. Escape hides it. data-shortcut="Ctrl+Z", or a trailing (Ctrl+Z) in the text, shows key caps. It is on once juel.js loads; juel.tips.disable() turns it off. Open with ?look&theme=dark for the dark look.
