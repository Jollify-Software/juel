Demo

juel.demo.play(script) plays a demo: a ghost hand moves to each control, opens menus, drags (into a scaled iframe, with pointer capture, and HTML drag-and-drop), types and presses keys, with real events, and a caption says what it's doing. The bar pauses, steps, changes speed, starts again or stops; your own clicks pause it. juel.demo.coach(script) is the same script for you to do: the target is ringed and each step waits for you. Open with ?look&theme=dark for the dark look.
