QR Code

<juel-qr value="..."> encodes a string (byte mode, UTF-8) and renders it as crisp, scalable SVG. ecl sets error-correction (L/M/Q/H), scale the pixels-per-module, margin the quiet-zone width. It fires qr-render on success and qr-error if the payload is empty or too large.

Basic

A URL at the default error-correction level M.

Error-correction levels

Same text, ecl L → H. Higher levels survive more damage but pack denser.

Colour

foreground / background — keep the contrast strong.
