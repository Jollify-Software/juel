Observer

<juel-observer> is a declarative IntersectionObserver — wrap anything in it and it toggles in-view-class (default in-view) on itself, and fires observer-enter/ observer-leave, whenever it crosses threshold. It has no opinion on what "in view" means: style the class yourself for a pure-CSS reveal, or wire a <juel-trigger> to the events to drive anything else — play a juel-animate, show a toast — no JS required. once stops observing after the first entrance instead of toggling back and forth.

Effect gallery

Every effect here is plain CSS keyed off .in-view — juel-observer has no idea any of it exists. The data-effect attribute is just this page's own naming convention, not something the component reads. None of these are once: scroll away and each one reverses, then plays again on the way back — each card is also staggered a little behind the one before it, so they cascade in rather than all firing together.
